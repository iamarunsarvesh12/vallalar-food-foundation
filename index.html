Our Story

Rooted in Heritage, Built for a Nation

What began as one flame in Tamil Nadu has grown into a Pan-India promise: that no one, anywhere, should go without a meal.

In 1867, Saint Vallalar (Ramalinga Swamigal) established an unextinguished kitchen flame — the Arutperunjothi — believing that feeding a hungry soul is the highest form of humanity. This act of Annadhanam, sacred food charity, and the timeless spirit of Virunthombal, gracious hospitality to every guest, remain the beating heart of our work today.

What was once a single kitchen is now a growing network of community kitchens, food-recovery patrols, and nutrition drives spanning states and cities across India — carrying the same flame forward, for every community that needs it.
